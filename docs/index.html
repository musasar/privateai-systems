Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our private AI solutions.

Is my data truly private?

Yes. Your AI runs entirely on your own infrastructure — no data is sent to cloud services, third-party APIs, or external servers. Everything stays within your office network.

Can it work without internet?

Yes. Local AI models operate fully offline. Some features like web search or external API integrations require internet, but the core AI functions without it.

Can you build the hardware?

Yes. We design, assemble, and configure custom AI workstations — or work with your existing hardware vendor in any European country.

Do you provide maintenance?

Yes. All packages include remote maintenance — updates, model upgrades, monitoring, and optimization. Enterprise includes priority SLA.

Can existing office computers be upgraded?

Yes. If your current hardware meets the requirements (GPU, RAM), we can upgrade it instead of replacing. We evalute on a case-by-case basis.

Which industries do you serve?

We specialize in data-sensitive sectors: legal firms, healthcare clinics, accounting offices, engineering companies, manufacturing, real estate, and government/defense.

How long does deployment take?

Typically 3 working days on-site: Day 1 — hardware setup + Linux, Day 2 — AI stack deployment, Day 3 — training + documentation + handover.
